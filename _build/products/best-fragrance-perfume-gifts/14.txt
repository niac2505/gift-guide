How To Use
Spritz on wrists, neck, and décolleté throughout the day to stay cheirosa. In Portuguese, this means you smell incredibly delicious. Sol tip: Spritz wherever the day takes you. Feeling bold? Layer two (or more) fragrances for an unforgettable scent experience.
