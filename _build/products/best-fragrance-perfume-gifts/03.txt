FAQ
What do each of the notes within Sunlit Vanilla smell like?
The top note of Sunlit Vanilla is Bergamot, a subtle and soft fresh citrus that beautifully blends the gourmand notes in the fragrance. The heart notes are Caramel and Coconut – think coconut macaroon, caramel drizzle, and a delicious crème brûlée. The base notes include Vanilla, Creamy Musk, Tonka Bean and Amber. These notes dry down to smell like vanilla ice cream, sandalwood and marzipan.

What makes Sunlit Vanilla different from traditional vanilla fragrances?
Sunlit Vanilla opens with fresh bergamot and melts into caramel and coconut, giving it a warm, sunlit sweetness instead of a one-note sugary feel. As it dries down, creamy musk, tonka bean, and amber add depth and softness, so the vanilla feels smooth, layered, and skin-like rather than overly sweet.

How should I store Sunlit Vanilla?
We recommend storing Sunlit Vanilla in a cool, dry place away from sunlight.
