How To Use
Spray the fragrance generously onto pulse points (neck, wrists, and chest) for a lasting scent that moves with you. Layer the fragrances to match your mood:
For a soft and calming scent: Layer Cashmere Muse with Blush Wood. Sweet, powdery florals melt into skin-like salted musk for an irresistible, gentle trail.
For a balanced contrast of light and dark: Layer Velvet Brew with Cashmere Muse. The rich intensity of coffee with salted musk notes offer alluring depth and airy lightness.
For an unexpected twist: Layer Blush Wood with Velvet Brew. Soft florals and rich coffee accord give a delicate-meets-decadent combination that's unexpected and unapologetic.
