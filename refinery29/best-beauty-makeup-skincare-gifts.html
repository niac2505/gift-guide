21 Beauty Gifts For The Friend Who Has Everything In Her Vanity

This holiday season, we’ve found the gifts that feel great to get and even better to give. Shop our collection of editor-approved holiday gift guides here.
Whether you’re looking for a stocking stuffer present or a luxury treat, shopping for the beauty enthusiast on your list can be a challenge. It may seem like they own every eyeshadow palette in existence, have already tried that trending lip liner, and yep — their bathroom is already overflowing with endless lotions and potions. (She basically invented the "everything shower." NBD.) But don't worry — with a little guidance (aka, us), you'll be able to dazzle even the most well-stocked beauty lover.
To help you in your quest for a beauty gift they’re sure to treasure, we’ve compiled the ultimate guide of the shiniest, sparkliest, most joy-sparking hair, makeup, skincare, fragrance, and more from brands ranging from e.l.f. Cosmetics to Oribe.
At Refinery29, we’re here to help you navigate this overwhelming world of stuff. All of our market picks are independently selected and curated by the editorial team. All product details reflect the price and availability at the time of publication. If you buy or click on something we link to on our site, Refinery29 may earn commission.

  • Hourglass Ambient Lighting Edit Unlocked Palette
    Hourglass' baked powders are industry-leading for a reason. They're not only gorgeous to look at, but blend like a dream to create dimensional, luminous skin with an otherworldly glow. These limited-edition palettes feature artwork by Ukrainian tattoo artist Sasha Unisex, making them a treasure for any beauty collector.
  • Timeline Mito-Biotic™ Skin Longevity Essentials
    Timeline’s Longevity Essentials Kit has everything your giftee needs for healthier, stronger skin. The brand’s Gentle Cleanser is a beauty-editor favorite for the way it melts makeup in seconds, while the dewy cream helps reinforce the skin barrier and turns up the glow. It’s a full skincare routine in one, housed in a chic vegan-leather pouch that’s worth $75 on its own.
  • e.l.f. Cosmetics Glow Reviver Plumping Lip Oil Kit
    e.l.f. pulled out all the stops this year with its gift sets, but if we had to choose just one, it would be this quartet of lip oils. Featuring a mix of rosy and neutrals, cream and shimmer finishes (including two hues you'll only find in this kit!), it's the perfect gift for the lip combo queen on your list.
  • Slip Pure Silk 4-Pack Skinny Scrunchie Ornament
    Fact: You can never own too many scrunchies — especially when they're made from luxurious, super-soft silk.
  • Nécessaire The Body Wash Collection Set
    The only thing better than Nécessaire's quiet luxury body wash? Four of them.
  • Oribe Gold Lust Collection
    There are “nice gifts,” and then there are the gifts that feel like a deep breath. Oribe’s Gold Lust collection is the latter. The Repair & Restore shampoo, conditioner, and hair oil transform even the quickest wash day into something luxe — and this year’s limited-edition box, wrapped in original artwork by Kustaa Saksi, makes it feel even more special.

  • Orebella Trio Travel Gift Set
    Bella Hadid’s Orebella Trio Travel Gift Set is a gift your favorite perfume lover will swoon over. Cozy enough for chilly winter mornings, festive enough for holiday parties, and light enough for post-holiday office wear, these perfumes are guaranteed to become their go-to everyday indulgence.
