25 Thoughtful Gift Ideas For The Mom Who Does It All

This holiday season, we’ve found the gifts that feel great to get and even better to give. Shop our collection of editor-approved holiday gift guides here.
From navigating our tantrums as toddlers to helping us through personal and professional heartbreaks as adults, is there anything the moms in our lives don't do for us? And while nothing could ever be enough for the woman who literally and figuratively gave us life, we're doing our best to come close with the best gift ideas that won't be repurposed for a work White Elephant or laid to rest in some drawer in your childhood home.
From reader-favorite brands like Lululemon, Jenni Kayne, Caraway, and more, we've curated a mix of covetable fashion, cozy home goods, beauty splurges, personalized trinkets, and many other treasures ahead. (We didn't forget about the picky moms out there, either, picking out practical items they'll appreciate and use for years to come, like gardening tools for the green-fingered or chic cookware for the mom who loves to host.)
All linked products are independently selected by our editors. If you purchase any of these products, we may earn a commission.
