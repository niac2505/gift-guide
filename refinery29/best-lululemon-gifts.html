When it comes to coveted pieces for the workout- and comfort-obsessed, Lululemon is the ultimate one-stop shop. From the brand's signature ultra-soft leggings designed for Pilates and lounging to an array of jackets for every outdoor activity, it's no surprise that the retailer is a reader favorite year-round — and a go-to destination for all things holiday gifting.
Lululemon’s gift shop is open for business, meaning you can get a curated guide to the best styles to ship for everyone on your list. Your besties will love the convenient (and very viral!) belt bags and iconic half-zips, while a cozy beanie and running sneakers might be the right fit for a boyfriend, dad, or brother. (And don't sleep on the retailer's newest collaborations with Erewhon — for the foodies — and Fanatics — for the sports lovers!) No matter what, Lululemon has gifts for everyone and for every budget.
While you're at it, why not treat yourself to something cozy and functional this winter, too? Check out the brand’s thermal leggings, puffer vests, wool slippers, and tons more new arrivals to brave the cold months ahead.
