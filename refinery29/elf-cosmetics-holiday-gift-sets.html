It may be early November, but go to any store and it's as if the holidays are single-digit days away. Case in point: Reader-favorite brand e.l.f. Cosmetics, which has made a name for its ridiculously good, ahead-of-the-curve products at accessible prices. From the editor-approved Halo Skin Tint SPF 50 to the viral Glow Reviver Plumping Lip Oils, the brand rarely misses — including when it comes to holiday offerings.
And just in time for gifting season, e.l.f. is dazzling beauty lovers with a suite of holiday sets at every price point.
