Luxury Travel Gifts For The Jet-Setter Who’s Always Booking Flights

This holiday season, we’ve found the gifts that feel great to get and even better to give. Shop our collection of editor-approved holiday gift guides here.
If there’s someone on your shopping list who lives for their next vacation (and maybe takes the saying "catch flights, not feelings" a bit too literally), you can’t go wrong with giving them a travel gift this holiday season. The challenge lies in sourcing the right present to support their passion — after all, they’ve probably seen everything on their adventures, and will have an equally discerning eye for weekender bags and airport outfits.
But, fret not, this is where our tingling shopping senses come in: We’ve searched high and low for the luxury travel gifts and packing must-haves that’ll make your giftee's next journey the five-star experience they deserve. No matter their preferred travel method or most frequent travel plans — from vacations to work trips — we’ve compiled a range of practical, luxurious, and thoughtful presents. Think everything from versatile carry-on bags, cozy neck pillows, and stylish earplugs for long-haul flights; hard-shell cases, travel-size skincare, and roomy toiletry bags for train journeys; along with chic travel journals, compressive packing cubes, and compact reusable bottles for road trips.
And while this list contains some of the most luxe gifts, many of the travel accessories included can be found on Amazon or cost under $50, proving that high quality doesn’t necessarily mean expensive or unattainable. Just order them ASAP so they arrive on time!
