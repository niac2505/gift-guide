Working on Your Christmas List? Here’s Somos’ Latine Holiday Gift Ideas For 2025

Nochebuena is just over a month away, and if you still have names on your holiday gift list you haven’t crossed off (first, same!), we’ve got you. The Somos team put together an extensive gift guide for every Latine in your life. Organized by beauty, fashion, home, wellness, kids, him, and stocking stuffers, it’s packed with Latine-owned goodies that will look adorable under your Christmas tree and are guaranteed to bring photo-worthy, cheesy smiles to your loved ones’ faces. And that’s what gift-giving is really about, isn’t it? Spreading sweet joy.

  • Greene is Gold's Mindy Heart Locket Necklace
    Well-made, thoughtfully designed jewelry that preserves moments and stories are the kinds of pieces that become heirlooms, and that’s exactly what Black Boricua designer Mikaela Greene creates through her brand Greene is Gold. Her handmade resin jewelry feels like wearable gardens. From locket necklaces to charm bracelets to wristwatches, each piece incorporates flowers Greene hand-picks from farms across the country. “I dry or press them by hand, then create a hand-mixed resin background that sets the tone for the colors and feeling of the piece,” Greene explains. “From there, I build in small layers, adding flowers intuitively, unless it’s a custom piece with specific requests." Greene is Gold’s ready-made pieces, like the Mindy stainless steel locket necklace, are beautiful and meaningful on their own. But you can also customize a piece to make it personal. “I’ve had people send in flowers from funerals, share their partner’s favorite colors down to the hex code, or even show me photos of themselves and ask me to make something with creative freedom that I think they’d like,” Greene says. “I’ve always been a lover girl, so getting to create something that helps people express care, connection, and emotion feels so fulfilling.”
