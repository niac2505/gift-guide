The Feeling
The first object of SIDIA Parfums. An heirloom, designed to keep your scent close.

The Design
Inspired by the architectural jewelry of our matriarch, Sidia. A weighted, gold-finished metal object engineered to house your refillable 10 mL Eau de Parfum.
Includes one 10 mL Eau de Parfum in the fragrance of your choice.

The Fragrance (Braless)
Smoky Leather, Vanilla & Skin Musk
BRALESS is warm and undone, with vanilla cream, coconut and soft woods that wear close and intimate. Lived-in like your favorite tee, soft as memory—sensual but grounding, intimate but never overpowering.

Top: Black Pepper · Spanish Rosemary · Coconut
Heart: Violet · Black Tea · Peony · Cedar · Orris
Base: Patchouli · Papyrus · Vanilla Cream · Musk · Leather

The Perfumer
Crafted alongside master perfumer Jérôme Epinette, whose intuitive approach to texture and memory helped shape each SIDIA fragrance.

The Origin
SIDIA began at a vanity filled with intentional, storied treasures. The Strike carries that philosophy forward, transforming fragrance into an object to keep, refill, and return to.
"We never set out to make another perfume. We wanted to create something you'd keep." — Erin Kleinberg
