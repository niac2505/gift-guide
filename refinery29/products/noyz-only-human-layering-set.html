How To Use
Eau de Parfum: Spray neck, wrists, chest, or wherever you feel a heart beat. Avoid contact with eyes.
Solid Fragrance: Glide over neck, wrists, chest, or wherever you feel a heartbeat. Avoid contact with eyes.
Mylk de Parfum: Let Mylk de Parfum melt where you want your scent to linger (pulse points, collarbones, the curve of your wrist). Wear it alone or layer under your favorite scent for amplified, extended fragrance.
Warning: If irritation occurs, stop use. We recommend a patch test before full application.
